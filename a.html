Services

Everything around the car.

Owning a car from this period is a long relationship. We stay useful for all of it.

  • i

    Sourcing

    Looking for one particular car? We search quietly, among private owners as well as the market, and tell you plainly what we find.

  • ii

    Consignment

    Your car shown as we show our own: lit properly, described honestly, seen only by serious buyers.

  • iii

    Storage

    Heated, secure storage with battery care and periodic running, by arrangement.

  • iv

    Enclosed transport

    Door to door in an enclosed carrier, arranged and tracked by us from collection to delivery.

  • v

    Inspection and restoration referrals

    Pre-purchase inspections, and introductions to the restorers and specialists we trust with our own cars.
