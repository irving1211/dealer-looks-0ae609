Lot 01

1955 Gullwing Coupé in silver

Factory coachwork: a steel body on a tubular spaceframe, the doors hinged at the roof. Silver, with a period-correct interior.

Chassis no. 198.040-55-00xxx · Engine no. 198.980-55-00xxx (placeholder numbers)

Few post-war road cars were drawn so directly from competition. The spaceframe beneath this coupé, light and very stiff, runs high along the sills, and the doors had to open upward from the roof to let anyone in at all. What began as an engineering compromise became the car’s signature.

This example is offered in silver, the colour in which the model is most often remembered. The six-cylinder engine is laid over on its side to lower the bonnet, and is fed by mechanical fuel injection, then a rarity in a road car. The file follows the car from Switzerland to France and later to New England, and it is offered with that file in full.

The car may be seen at Foundry Row by appointment. A specialist will take you round it, open the file with you, and answer what the file does not.

Fixed-head coupé coachwork, on wire wheels. A period colour that reads grey in shade and blue in sun.

Lot 03

1956 Gullwing Coupé in Signal red

Factory coachwork, as Lot 01. Finished in red, a rarer choice than silver when these cars were new.

Services24

Services to collectors

i.Finding a particular car

If the car you want is not in this catalogue, tell us which one, in what colour, with what kind of history. Many good cars change hands without being advertised. We look there, and we report back honestly, including when the answer is to wait.

ii.Consignment

Collection cars offered by private treaty: catalogued, photographed and shown by appointment, on terms agreed in writing first.

iii.Storage

Heated, secure storage at Foundry Row. Batteries kept, tyres turned, cars run by arrangement.

iv.Enclosed transport

Cars travel in closed carriers with drivers we know: to viewings, to shows, and home.

v.Inspection and restoration

We arrange independent pre-purchase inspections, and refer owners to restorers and marque specialists whose work we have seen at close quarters.

vi.The history file

Every car we offer comes with its file read, indexed and summarised, gaps included. You see what we know and what we do not.
