For our clients

What we look after for you

Ask for one thing or all of them. Either way, it's one conversation with one person.

  1. i.

    Finding a particular car On request

    Tell us the car, the colour and the kind of history you want. We search quietly, including cars that never come to market, and we'll say so when the right answer is to wait.

  2. ii.

    Consignment By agreement

    We present your car to our clients, handle every enquiry and viewing, and keep you informed in plain words.

  3. iii.

    Climate-controlled storage Monthly

    Kept dry at an even temperature, on a battery tender, started and moved on a schedule, and ready when you call.

  4. iv.

    Enclosed transport Quoted

    Door to door in a closed carrier, insured in transit. We tell you when it leaves and when it arrives.

  5. v.

    Inspections Arranged

    An independent pre-purchase inspection by a specialist in the model, with the report sent to you exactly as written.

  6. vi.

    Restoration referrals Introductions

    Introductions to restorers and trimmers whose work we know, and someone to keep an eye on the job for you.

  7. vii.

    Paperwork Included

    Title, registration, import papers and the history file, put in order and kept together.
